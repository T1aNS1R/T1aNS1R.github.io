# About me
- I am currently a researcher in Beijing, China. Before that, I earned my PhD from Chinese Academy of Sciences, where I was mentored by Prof. [Xian Sun](https://scholar.google.com.hk/citations?user=xWKPHDYAAAAJ&hl=zh-CN).

- Research interests: Design, Optimization, and Application of Large Language Models and Multimodal Large Models.
